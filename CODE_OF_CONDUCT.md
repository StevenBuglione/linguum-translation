# Code of conduct

Linguum Translation contributors must communicate respectfully, review evidence and ideas on their merits, protect private or security-sensitive information, and maintain a harassment-free environment.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, personal attacks, and sustained disruption of technical collaboration. Repository maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled as confidentially and promptly as practical.
